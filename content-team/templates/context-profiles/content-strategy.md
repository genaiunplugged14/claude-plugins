# Content Strategy

> **Purpose**: Tells the team what we publish, how, and what's already on the calendar. The writer structures every draft to these standards; the reviewer checks against them; the researcher cross-checks the calendar so we don't research a topic that's already planned or shipped.
> **Update cadence**: Monthly. Update the calendar block every month. Update pillars/format only when your content positioning shifts.

## Brand Voice (summary - full rules in `brand-voice.md`)

The writer and reviewer both read `brand-voice.md` for the complete rules and the forbidden-phrases list. The short version:

- **Experienced friend, not a brochure.** "I've driven Manali to Leh 12 times. Here's what changed in 2026." NOT "This scenic highway offers panoramic views."
- **Specific numbers, always.** Distances in km, altitudes in feet, prices in Rs, fuel stops by km marker, dhabas by name.
- **Honest about difficulty.** Bad roads, altitude sickness, overpriced hotels - call it straight. Don't sanitize for sponsorship.
- **Hindi/Punjabi naturally mixed in.** dhaba, chai, maggi, nallah, raasta, gaon - the word the road locals use.
- **Safety-first.** Always name the road hazards, weather risks, acclimatization needs.
- **Budget-conscious.** Real prices from actual trips, not sponsored stays.

## Content Format Standards (the writer builds every draft to this)

- **Length**: 2,000-4,000 words for route guides; 1,500-2,500 for how-tos
- **Practical Info Box** at the top: best time to visit, difficulty, budget range, nearest ATM/fuel, mobile network
- **Quick Answer block** after every H2/H3 (40-60 words, AEO-friendly - the bit an AI assistant can quote)
- **5-8 FAQ entries** drawn from "People Also Ask"
- **5-10 internal links** to related routes/guides per post
- **First-person voice** throughout - never "this region offers", always "I drove"
- **Year in the title** for any route/permit/status content where freshness matters (which is most of it)
- **Real proof**: at least one specific dated detail only a person who went there would know

## SEO / AEO Focus

- **Target People Also Ask** - answer the PAA questions for the keyword inside the post
- **Long-tail over short-tail** - "manali to spiti via atal tunnel 2026" not "spiti trip"
- **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)** - clear questions + scannable answers so AI assistants retrieve and cite us
- **Seasonal timing** - publish 8 weeks before peak travel season for the route in question

## Topics We Cover (priority order)

1. Road trip planning + multi-day itineraries
2. Pass conditions + opening/closing dates
3. Permit requirements (Inner Line, forest, military)
4. Budget breakdowns with real prices
5. Vehicle preparation (car AND motorcycle)
6. Altitude sickness - prevention, symptoms, treatment
7. Solo and small-group travel logistics
8. Local food and accommodation (dhaba culture)
9. Photography + GPS coordinate sharing
10. Off-season travel windows

---

## Topics We Down-Rank or Skip

- International travel (out of scope)
- Luxury / resort / package tourism (wrong audience)
- Generic "top 10 places" without first-hand experience
- Pure trekking content (IndiaHikes owns this space)
- Political content about border regions (legal/safety risk)
