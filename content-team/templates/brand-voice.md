# Brand Voice Rules - Discover With Dheeraj

This file is read by the writer (to draft in voice) and the reviewer (to audit against voice).
It is the detailed companion to the voice summary in `context-profiles/content-strategy.md`.

## Voice profile

- **Audience**: budget Indian Himalayan road trippers - first-timers (Avatar A) and returning adventure travelers (Avatar B). See `context-profiles/business-context.md`.
- **Tone**: an experienced friend who has driven these roads, telling you the truth so you stay safe and don't overpay.
- **Register**: practical, specific, honest about difficulty. Inspirational second, useful first.

## Structure rules

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| First sentence | Under 12 words. Concrete, not "Welcome to". Creates curiosity without clickbait. |
| Paragraphs | Never more than 4 lines. |
| Sentences | Average 20 words. Split anything over 30. |
| Numbers | Every claim needs a real number or named place (km, feet, Rs, dhaba name, km marker). |
| Voice | First person for experience ("I drove"), second person for the reader ("you'll need"). Active voice. |
| Local words | Use the road word naturally: dhaba, chai, maggi, nallah, raasta, gaon, sheher. Don't translate to dictionary English. |

## Required elements (every route guide / how-to)

- A first sentence that places you on the road, not at a desk
- **Practical Info Box** near the top (best time, difficulty, budget range, nearest fuel/ATM, mobile network)
- **Quick Answer block** (40-60 words) after each major section - the part an AI assistant can quote
- At least one **first-hand proof** detail (a dated observation, a named dhaba, a real price paid)
- Honest **safety / difficulty** call-out where the route warrants it
- **5-8 FAQ entries** pulled from People Also Ask
- A real **budget line** in rupees (fuel-only or DIY, not package pricing)

## Forbidden phrases (instant reject - the brochure voice)

```
nestled, breathtaking, hidden gem, must-visit, must-see, paradise on earth,
bucket list, picturesque, scenic beauty, panoramic views, a feast for the eyes,
unparalleled, pristine, majestic, enchanting, mesmerizing, gateway to,
offers something for everyone, leaves you spellbound, words cannot describe,
this region offers, a perfect blend of
```

## Numbers and freshness

- Put the **year in the title** for anything seasonal (routes, permits, road status).
- Prices in **Rs**, distances in **km**, altitudes in **feet**.
- If a fact is from a prior season, say so ("as of June 2026" / "needs current confirmation") - never imply old info is current.

## What "good" sounds like

Good: "The Atal Tunnel cut five hours off the old Rohtang route. I drove it in June 2026."
Bad: "Nestled in the majestic Himalayas, Spiti is a breathtaking paradise that belongs on every bucket list."

Good: "Diesel at Tandi is the last pump for 365 km. Fill up. There is no second chance."
Bad: "Ensure adequate fuel as petrol stations may be limited in this scenic region."

Good: "Day one ends at Kaza. Rs 800 got me a clean dhaba room with hot water."
Bad: "Accommodation options are available to suit every budget and preference."
